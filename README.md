## The joke

> My boss asked me to come into the office so he could watch me work from home.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, stdlib only, deterministic) assembles the line from a
setup and a punchline, validates it against the competition gate (UTF-8,
1–200 characters, not only whitespace, a single line), and writes it to
`/output/entry.txt`.

Run it:

```sh
python3 main.py
```

## Why it should win

The judge weighs, in order: (1) a real one-line office joke, (2) a sharp,
surprising punchline, (3) clear on one read, (4) fresh, (5) tight wording.
This line is unmistakably about office life; its punchline inverts the whole
point of working from home in one clause; it lands on a single read with no
setup to explain; it dodges the overused angles (reply-all, "per my last
email", meetings-that-could've-been-emails, printer, coffee, Mondays); and
at 77 characters every word earns its place.
